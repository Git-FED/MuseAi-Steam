# FAQ

**Can Muse lock a game at the cap?** No. Steam does not provide a remote hard lock. Muse warns at 75%, 90%, and 100% and logs overruns.

**Will Muse buy a sale game?** No. Only Git-FED's exact order can authorize a purchase.

**Does this cover PlayStation and Pokemon?** Yes, as separate library records. PS4 history is in `data/playstation/`; Pokemon ROM hacks are in `data/pokemon/`, grouped as an emulator-only block with no Steam hours.

**Where do saves go?** Git-FED's stated destination is the Google Drive `tatp770` folder. The local index lives in `data/saves/`. Binary `.sav` files are cataloged, not interpreted as guaranteed in-game progress.
